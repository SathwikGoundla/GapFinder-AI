# Sample Past Paper: PLC Fundamentals (DEMO CONTENT)

Original practice questions written for the GapFinder AI demo.

## Section A: Short Questions
1. Name the four stages of the PLC scan cycle in order.
2. Explain why a 4 to 20 mA signal is preferred over 0 to 20 mA.
3. A sourcing proximity sensor is connected to a sourcing input card. What happens and why?
4. What is the difference between a TON and an RTO timer?

## Section B: Ladder Logic
5. Draw a start/stop motor circuit with a seal-in contact. Explain the role of each contact.
6. An NC contact in the program is addressed to a physical NC stop button. The button is not pressed. Is the rung true or false? Explain.
7. A counter's rung stays true for 5 seconds. How many counts are added? Explain.

## Section C: Safety
8. Why must an emergency stop not rely only on the PLC program?
9. Describe one risk of forcing outputs while the machine is running.
